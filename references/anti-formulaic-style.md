# Anti-formulaic academic style

The aim is sharper thought and a recognizable authorial voice. This is not detector evasion.

## High-risk patterns

Inspect clusters, not isolated occurrences.

### Manufactured binaries

- `不是/并非/不再……而是……`
- `不仅……而且/更……`
- `not merely/not only X, but Y`
- `rather than being X, it is Y`

Ask whether X and Y truly exclude each other. If they coexist, state proportion, sequence, condition, or mechanism. If the correction is real, keep it concise and rare.

### Ceremonial openings and endings

- `随着……的不断发展`, `在……背景下`, `近年来受到广泛关注`
- `综上所述`, `由此可见`, `总而言之`
- `具有重要理论意义和实践价值`, `为……提供新的视角/路径`
- `in today's rapidly evolving landscape`, `this underscores/highlights the importance`

Replace with the event, date, dispute, gap, finding, or consequence that makes the sentence necessary. Delete endings that merely restate the paragraph.

### Empty intensification

Flag stacked praise and scale words: `深刻`, `显著`, `重要`, `关键`, `复杂`, `多维`, `全面`, `系统`, `创新性`, `pivotal`, `profound`, `intricate`, `transformative`, `robust`, `holistic`. Keep only when a defined comparison or measurement supports them.

### Excessive navigation

Repeated `首先/其次/此外/最后`, `值得注意的是`, `需要指出的是`, and `furthermore/moreover/notably` can substitute for logic. Express the actual relation or let sentence order carry it.

### Symmetry without inquiry

Watch for identical paragraph lengths, obligatory three-part lists, repeated conclusions, balanced pros-and-cons without stakes, and uniform sentence openings. Break symmetry only by improving the argument, not by randomizing punctuation.

### Vague agency and nominal fog

Interrogate `实现了……的提升`, `促进了……的发展`, `进行了……的探索`, `赋能`, `建构`, `呈现出`, and long English nominalizations. Name who did what, to which object, by what operation, and with what observed result.

## Revision operations

1. Identify the sentence's factual or argumentative payload.
2. Remove unsupported evaluation and duplicated framing.
3. Restore the agent, operation, object, evidence, and boundary.
4. Choose the relation actually present: cause, condition, concession, sequence, comparison, or coexistence.
5. Rebuild sentence rhythm around information priority.
6. Re-read the paragraph for voice and continuity.

Do not perform synonym roulette, inject anecdotes, add fake imperfections, or make prose colloquial merely to look human.

## Compact examples

Formulaic: `数字媒介不仅改变了建筑表达，而且重塑了建筑本体。`

Specific: `实时渲染把材质调整移入方案评审现场；在三次记录的评审中，设计团队据此修改了立面开孔率。该证据说明决策时序发生变化，尚不足以证明建筑“本体”被重塑。`

Formulaic: `This study does not merely examine interfaces; it offers a transformative framework for spatial experience.`

Specific: `The study compares cursor trajectories and interview accounts from two museum interfaces. The resulting framework describes three recurring transitions between screen action and gallery movement; its transfer beyond museums remains untested.`
