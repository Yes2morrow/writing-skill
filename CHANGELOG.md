# Changelog

## 1.1.0 — 2026-08-25

- Added a held-out set of 10 synthetic no-skill papers and 300 bilingual paragraphs, raising the stored synthetic language corpus to 660 source cases and 1,320 comparison paragraphs.
- Profiled 205 local scholarly PDFs; retained copyright-safe metrics for 85 extractable documents (68 Chinese, 17 English) and visually checked representative English and Chinese layouts.
- Added local-corpus comparison tools and a prose-finish module focused on precision, information contour, cadence, concrete spatial/operational verbs, and restraint.
- Expanded language diagnostics for abstract shells, modifier stacks, empty transitions, echo conclusions, and overclaims; fixed Chinese numeric-anchor detection.
- Added a 300-case held-out paired check: 228 lower-risk skill references, 72 ties, 0 losses, with a −7.0-point mean configured-risk delta. This automated result is not a human quality or authorship score.
- Kept article structure, research logic, and evidence creation outside the skill's scope.
